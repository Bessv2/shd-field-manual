01 Foundations

The three core attributes and six specializations every build in this manual is built around. If you already know these, skip to the workflow or the builds.

Red Horizon changes to track: the Determined talent was reworked to behave like Perfect Determined — it no longer chains indefinitely off Headhunter, and endless body-shot-to-headshot conversion now lives on the Iron Will exotic chest instead. Brand Sets lost their Shock Resistance / Health / Incoming Repairs / Swap Speed bonuses in favor of Protection from Elites, and every weapon-type brand now grants a damage bonus at both 1 and 2 pieces. Red Horizon is also the last season planned to add new Brand Sets, so the roster below is close to final.

Core Attributes

Red — Weapon Damage

Everything that makes your gun hit harder.

  • Critical Hit Chance / Damage
  • Headshot Damage
  • Weapon-archetype damage (AR, LMG, SMG…)
Backbone of every DPS build. Chase Crit Chance to ~50–60% before stacking Crit Damage.

Blue — Armor

Everything that keeps you standing.

  • Total Armor / Armor Regeneration
  • Incoming Repairs
  • Hazard, Explosive, Disrupt & Shock Resistance
Priority for tanks and frontline hybrids; a minimum floor on every build so you can survive Heroic/Legendary one-shots.

Yellow — Skill Tier

Everything that powers your skills.

  • Skill Tier itself (caps at 6, gates mod slots)
  • Skill Damage / Haste / Duration
  • Repair Skills, Skill Health, Skill Efficiency
Backbone of skill and support builds. Lock Skill Tier 6 first, then split Haste/Damage/Duration by what your skills need.

03 Global Modifiers

Red Horizon replaced the old Recombinant Modifiers system's module/stack/firmware math with Global Modifiers — a fast, cover-centric "Under Pressure" loop built around one universal Pressure Gauge that layers on top of whatever gear set or loadout you're already running; you don't need a specific gear piece or gear set to use it.

The core loop: build a Pressure Gauge from 0% to 100% in combat. There are no cooldown timers on seasonal abilities anymore — the gauge itself is the "currency" that fuels them: it climbs on kills, pays out automatic passive bonuses as it climbs, and lets you fire off one of three Active Modifiers once it crosses 90%. Using an Active Modifier drains the gauge back to 0%. There are passive modifiers that change how the Pressure Guage works.

Decay: stay out in the open too long without a kill and the gauge drains rapidly. Sitting in cover, chaining cover-to-cover moves, or simply securing a kill pauses the decay — so the loop rewards working behind cover between kills rather than face-tanking in the open.

Countering them: fire is the cleanser. Setting an enemy on fire permanently purges all of its Hostile Modifiers for the rest of that fight, so keeping an incendiary weapon or fire skill on hand is worth it even in builds that don't otherwise lean on status effects.
Open world — the D.C. Invasion: the Cleaners have migrated south from NYC to Washington D.C. this season, ambushing open-world activities, hijacking Control Points, and sending an elite squad called Company 95 after you. The D.C. skybox turns a hazy red as the seasonal narrative escalates — mostly flavor, but worth knowing before it catches you off guard mid-farming-run.

07 Best PvP Builds

Dark Zone and Conflict loadouts. Conflict and Toxic DZ normalize a lot of PvE power, so brand-set consistency and talent uptime usually beat raw gear-set numbers there — Blackout DZ below is the exception.

Toxic vs. Blackout Dark Zone: Red Horizon split the DZ into two modes. Toxic DZ is the permanent, always-on zone — stats are normalized to a level-40 floor, so it's low-risk solo PvE farming for contaminated loot with only occasional PvP threat. Blackout DZ rotates weekly against the classic DZ and is the opposite: no normalization, so your full SHD Watch, Expertise and Prototype power carries in, Global PvP Balance still tunes weapons/gear/skills/Augments on top of that, and Black Tusk is the dominant hostile faction. Blackout also drops better loot and higher Exotic rates. If "endgame PvP" is the goal, build for Blackout weeks and expect real player threat; the builds below skew toward that full-power assumption.
